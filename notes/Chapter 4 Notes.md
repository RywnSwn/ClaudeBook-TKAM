## Character Tracker (STEAL)
### Jem
- Ch 4 - "Don't you know you're not supposed to even touch the trees over there? You'll get killed if you do!"
  STEAL category: Speech. Technique: dialogue. Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Private reaction to Scout, not performance for an audience, shows Jem's fear of the Radley place is genuine even though he'll later dare himself into touching the house.

- Ch 4 - decides to hold the pennies until school starts so they can ask around, instead of keeping or spending them.
  STEAL category: Actions. Technique: action-on-page + dialogue. Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Thinks it through logically and fairly rather than acting on impulse, treats "finders keepers" as not applying once money's involved.

- Ch 4 - pushes the tire "with all the force in his body" after being quietly "offended" earlier and "patiently awaiting an opportunity to reward me" (Scout had contradicted him on Hot Steams).
  STEAL category: Actions. Technique: action-on-page (narrator names the motive). Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Holds onto being contradicted longer than he lets on, settles it physically instead of saying anything.

- Ch 4 - after taunting Scout for not going back for the tire, "treaded water at the gate, then dashed in and retrieved" it himself.
  STEAL category: Actions. Technique: action-on-page. Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Right after mocking her fear, his own hesitation at the gate shows his fear of the place is just as real, pride is the only thing pushing him through it.

### Dill
- Ch 4 - "I helped the engineer for a while," met immediately by Jem's "In a pig's ear you did, Dill. Hush."
  STEAL category: Speech. Technique: dialogue. Direct/indirect: indirect.

### Scout
- Ch 4 - "Don't you believe a word he says, Dill... Calpurnia says that's nigger-talk."
  STEAL category: Speech. Technique: dialogue. Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Same pattern as the "just a Cunningham" line from Chapter 3, Scout repeating language she's absorbed from her environment without weighing it, not deliberate cruelty but worth tracking as a recurring pattern.

- Ch 4 - closing line: "There was more to it than he knew, but I decided not to tell him," after revealing she heard someone laughing inside the Radley house when the tire crashed into the yard.
  STEAL category: Thoughts (Scout-as-narrator). Technique: narration. Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Retrospective narrator holds back a real, private fear even from Jem in the moment, early hint something about this scene is different from ordinary kid superstition.

### Atticus
- Ch 4 - catches the Boo Radley game and asks "Does this by any chance have anything to do with the Radleys?" then "I hope it doesn't."
  STEAL category: Speech. Technique: dialogue. Direct/indirect: indirect.
  Consistent with his Chapter 1 stance (mind your own business about the Radleys), clearly suspects exactly what they're doing but chooses a quiet warning over grilling them.
