# Assignment: TKAM Character Chart

## What it is
Teacher-issued, running document across the whole unit (not tied to one chapter
test, not the end-of-quarter summative either, a third separate track).
Confirmed via a chart the user got in class on 2026-08-17.

## Format
Table with one row per character, filled in as the book progresses:
Atticus Finch, Scout Finch, Jem Finch, Dill, Boo Radley, Tom Robinson,
Mayella Ewell, Bob Ewell.

Columns:
1. **Character Traits** - what kind of person are they
2. **Evidence from Novel** - quotations or moments, with page #
3. **Characterization Strategy** - Narration, Dialogue, Actions, or Others' Views
4. **Author's Purpose** - why this trait matters to the story or themes

Teacher's own example already filled in for Jem: "Ego Driven," evidence being
Jem touching the Radley house after taunts (tied to the roof-jump flashback),
strategy: Action.

## How this maps onto our STEAL workflow
Columns 1-3 line up almost directly with what we already log per chapter:
- Character Traits = our "what it reveals" line
- Evidence = our quote
- Characterization Strategy = our technique label (their "Others' Views" =
  our Effect on others STEAL category, just named differently)

Column 4, Author's Purpose, is new. It's not automatic just because a trait
exists, it's a judgment call about whether a given moment connects to a
bigger story/theme or is more just character color/texture. Some moments
will be minor and that's a legitimate answer too, not everything needs to
be inflated into a big thematic claim.
